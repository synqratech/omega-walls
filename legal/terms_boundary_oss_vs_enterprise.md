# OSS vs Enterprise Legal Boundary

Document version: v0.1  
Status: Draft  
Last updated: 2026-05-13  
Owner: Founder / Product Security  
Legal status: Informational boundary summary. Binding obligations are defined only in signed contracts.

## 1. Product Boundary

`Omega Walls OSS` is provided under Apache-2.0.

`Omega Walls Enterprise` includes additional commercial features, support, and legal terms under separate agreements.

## 2. Feature-Flag Caveat

Some capabilities may be technically accessible behind feature flags in OSS builds for evaluation/testing.

Technical availability does not imply commercial support entitlement.

## 3. Support Boundary

OSS support is community/best-effort unless separately contracted.

Enterprise support levels, response targets, and service terms are defined in order forms and enterprise support documentation.

## 4. Contracting Entity and Terms

Contracting entity, governing law, and venue are **TBD until commercial signing**.

No legal commitment is formed by repository documentation alone.

## 5. Claims Discipline

No unsupported compliance certifications should be inferred from OSS availability.
Only explicitly stated and verifiable claims apply.
