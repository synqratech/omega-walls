## 1. Role Model

Omega Walls is designed primarily for self-hosted deployments.

In typical self-hosted mode, vendor systems do not process customer runtime content by default.

Where vendor receives customer-submitted support or incident artifacts, role allocation can be:

- Vendor as Processor for customer-provided operational data
- Vendor as Controller for account, billing, and support-contact data

Final role allocation is defined by contract and deployment model.

## 2. Transfer Triggers

Customer data transfer to vendor systems may occur only when one of the following is explicitly enabled:

- support submission
- incident review request
- telemetry export
- managed service scope

## 3. Data Categories (High Level)

- Customer Data (runtime/business content submitted by customer)
- Service Data (diagnostic metadata and counters)
- Business Contact Data (account/support communications)

## 4. International Transfer Mechanisms

Where required, contractual transfer mechanisms (for example SCC/UK transfer addendum placeholders) are handled via enterprise DPA templates.

## 5. DPA Availability

A DPA template is available on request for enterprise/pilot procurement workflows.

## 6. Subprocessors (Baseline)

For self-hosted mode, subprocessors are generally not used for customer content processing by default.
Any customer-data subprocessors in managed workflows are documented via enterprise annexes.
