## Q2. Is this SaaS or self-hosted?

Default model is self-hosted / customer-controlled.
Managed scope is only by explicit agreement.

## Q3. Do you receive raw customer prompts/documents by default?

No, not by default.

## Q4. Do you support DPA/MSA/SLA paperwork?

Yes, enterprise draft templates are available for pilot/commercial workflows.

## Q5. Which compliance certifications are currently claimed?

No unsupported certification claims are made by default.
Any formal certification claim requires explicit, verifiable evidence.

## Q6. How is vulnerability disclosure handled?

See `SECURITY.md` and `security/vulnerability_management.md`.

## Q7. What telemetry is collected?

Telemetry is configuration-driven and should avoid raw customer content and secrets by default.

## Q8. How fast can standard procurement/security questions be answered?

A baseline response package is intended to support first-pass responses within one business day for common pilot questions.
